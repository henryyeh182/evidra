## Boundary of the evidence

The packets support the existence and direction of conservative adaptation rules. They do not validate Pacevera's readiness or muscle-fatigue composites, ACWR 1.4 threshold, detraining thresholds 14/25/42/60, return factor 0.6, or any other internal cut point. EVD-R-001 and EVD-R-002 remain `internal_composite` with `studyDesign: none`; EP-005 is contextual literature, not validation of those scores. No decision effect, threshold, Rule ID, decision output, `decisionBasis`, `decisionId`, or `explain_decision` behavior was intentionally changed.
